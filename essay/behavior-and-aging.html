What Behavioral Patterns Can Reveal

Conventional behavioral measures provide valuable insights into an animal’s internal state. However, in the era of machine learning and computational modelling, behavioral recording is a valuable non-invasive method that can reveal many hidden gems. A recent study used the African turquoise killifish as an animal model to study aging, taking advantage of its short median lifespan of 4–8 months.

The researchers recorded the animals from adolescence until death and used a Gaussian hidden Markov model (GHMM) to divide their behavior into short, recurring movement patterns called “syllables.” They found that young and aged animals largely shared the same behavioral repertoire, but differed in how they used these syllables.

Moreover, they trained a machine learning classifier on behavioral patterns from young animals to predict whether they would have short or long lifespans, achieving over 70% accuracy. This means that an animal’s present behavior can carry information about its future life trajectory!
